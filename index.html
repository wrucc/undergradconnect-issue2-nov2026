From the Desk of the UCC

💙💛 CELEBRATING 104 YEARS,
WR UNDERGRADUATES & ADVISORS! 💛💙

As we celebrate 104 years of Sigma Gamma Rho this Founders Day season, I keep coming back to who our Founders were: seven young educators who believed scholarship, sisterhood, and service could change lives. What they built has lasted because they prepared, followed through, and led with purpose. That is the spirit of this issue.

This issue's theme is Finish Strong. Lead Forward. Prepare for What's Next.
Finish this semester strong in the classroom. Lead forward in your chapters with integrity and accountability. And prepare now for a productive Spring 2027.

There is so much to celebrate this season: new Sorors, step show champions, our first UCDF grant recipients, and new scholarship opportunities for undergraduates and advisors. You will find all of it inside, along with the tools to keep your chapter ready.

The Western Region is not simply asking you to meet requirements. We are investing in you as capable, confident, accountable women and leaders.

Finish strong, rest well, and come back ready.
I am proud of you, and I am here for you.

BEYOND THE LETTERS: GROWING INTO THE WOMAN YOU'RE BECOMING

Leadership Is More Than Holding a Position

A title does not make you a leader. Leadership shows up in how you prepare, how you communicate, whether you follow through, and how you hold yourself accountable when no one is checking. Some of the strongest leaders in your chapter may not hold a position at all.

REFLECT: Think about one responsibility you have taken on this semester. What did it teach you about leadership, and what will you do differently next semester?

Proposed for 2027: The Leadership Lab: You Make the Call, Sister to CEO, and Your Voice, Your Influence. Dates will be shared once confirmed.

Chapter Success Toolkit

Keep Your Chapter Ready

01

Chapter Report & Goals Tracker

FROM THE OFFICE OF THE UCC · MAKE IT ROUTINE

Your tracker is more than a report. It supports planning, accountability, reporting, and day-to-day chapter organization. Open it monthly, log your progress, and keep your goals and member roster current.

Pro tip: the Informal Rush & Performance tab calculates your submission deadlines from your event dates. Use it before you put anything on the calendar.
02

Informal Rush & Performance Submissions

SUBMIT AT LEAST 14 DAYS BEFORE YOUR EVENT

Every Informal Rush and performance request must be submitted a minimum of 14 days before the event, with all required signatures. If corrections are requested, make them promptly. Incomplete or unsigned requests will be returned, which can delay approval.

PLEASE NOTE: Submitting a request does not constitute approval. Chapters may not advertise or conduct any activity that requires approval until authorization has been received.

Informal Rush Request Form
Performance Review Submission Form
03

Membership & Financial Readiness

START NOW · BEFORE SPRING

Before the semester ends:

✔ Verify each member's current financial standing
✔ Review your chapter's Poodle Portal information
✔ Update chapter officer roles
✔ Address dues early through Billhighway

Local, regional, and international obligations must be satisfied as applicable. Poodle Portal updates can take 24–48 hours after payment, so do not wait until the last minute.
04

Spring 2027 T.O.R.C.H.: Compliance Comes First

*REQUIRED · ALL CHAPTERS CONSIDERING SPRING T.O.R.C.H.

If your chapter is considering Spring 2027 T.O.R.C.H., make sure your chapter is fully compliant now. Review your membership, academic, financial, reporting, and advisor requirements well before the schedule below. A chapter that is not compliant will not be able to move forward.

Complete the WR T.O.R.C.H. Compliance form before submitting your calendar. Need an "out-of-wave" calendar? Email the PDF to ucc@westernsgrho.org at least seven (7) days before the submission deadline, and before you submit the compliance form.
